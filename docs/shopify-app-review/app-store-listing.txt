Patrik International — Partner Catalog Sync

Tagline:
Always-on sync with the official Patrik International catalog: stock, prices, content and images

Description:
Connect your store to the Patrik International partner portal and keep listings
in sync with the authorized catalog. Stock, prices, titles, descriptions, tags
and images flow from your approved Patrik International range to the matching
products. Items match by SKU (barcode fallback); unmatched products are created
automatically. Choose stock-only or full content sync, and set location,
pricelist, VAT and sales channels. Once connected it just runs — always-on and
always up to date.

Features:
Always-on sync of stock, prices, titles, descriptions, tags and images
Matches products by SKU with barcode fallback; auto-creates unmatched items
Stock-only or full content sync, configured per connected store
Set location, pricelist priority, VAT handling and sales channels per store
Pulls only your authorized catalog, with Patrik International as the vendor
