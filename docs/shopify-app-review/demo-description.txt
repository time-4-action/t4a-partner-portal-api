Time 4 Action Product Sync – Shopify App Demo

Time 4 Action Product Sync connects to your Shopify store and performs a one-way
push of stock, products, prices, descriptions and images from the portal into
Shopify. The sync is non-destructive — nothing is ever deleted from your store.

In this demo we install the app, connect a Shopify store with a single OAuth
approval, point a data source at a Shopify location, choose what to sync, run a
sync, and verify the products in the Shopify admin.

What gets synced:
• Stock – live inventory quantities
• New products – created in your store
• Prices – kept in step with your pricelists
• Descriptions, tags and images

Key points:
• One secure OAuth install — no API keys to copy.
• One-way push only (portal → Shopify). Your Shopify edits stay safe.
• Connect multiple stores and sync automatically or on demand.
