SHOPIFY APP — TESTING INSTRUCTIONS

OVERVIEW
This app is the install bridge for our Export Portal. It does a one-way push of
stock, products, prices, descriptions and images from the portal into your Shopify
store. Nothing is ever deleted from your store. A test account with a source is
already configured for you.

LOGIN (you'll be asked for these during the install, see step 2)
- Username: demo
- Password: password is provided via login details

INSTALL (auto-connect via the link)
1. Open the install link in your browser. You're taken straight to Shopify's
   app install/consent screen — click "Install". (No store domain entry needed.)
2. You land on a short welcome page that explains the portal. Click
   "Sign in to connect" and log in with the credentials above. (A merchant who
   isn't a partner can instead use "Request access" on this page to ask for an
   account.)
3. You're returned to the Shopify Integration page and the store connects
   automatically — a green "Store connected successfully." banner shows and the
   store appears as a pill marked "Active". (No store domain entry needed — the
   link connects automatically.)

AFTER CONNECT — SET UP THE SYNC
4. Scroll to "Sources & locations".
5. In the source row, open the "Source" dropdown and pick the pre-configured
   source "Patrik · Demo accessories". If no row exists, click "Add source" first.
6. In the "Shopify location" dropdown, choose where products should be stocked.
7. Click "Configure" on that row. In the modal:
   - "How much should we manage?": pick "Create, then leave it to you" (recommended).
   - "What to sync": enable Stock, New products and Prices.
   - "Pricing": confirm pricelist order and VAT mode ("Includes/Excludes VAT").
   - "Sales channels": tick where new products publish (e.g. Online Store).
   - Click "Save changes".
8. If a bottom bar shows "Unsaved changes", click its "Save changes" too.

RUN & VERIFY
9. At the top, click "Sync now" (shows "Syncing…" while it runs).
10. Watch "Sync activity" — the Synced/Pending/Error counts and the recent-runs
    table update. Click a run for its detail.
11. In Shopify admin > Products: pushed items appear with titles, images and
    descriptions; open a product to confirm variants, prices and inventory at the
    chosen location.

OPTIONAL
- "Add store": connect a second store; click a store pill to switch.
- "Needs attention": lists any variant that couldn't be matched, with a reason.
- "Disconnect" then "Yes, disconnect": stops syncing; Shopify products stay intact.

NOTES FOR THE REVIEWER
- Install starts OAuth immediately; after you approve on Shopify you reach a
  welcome page, sign in, and the store then connects on its own.
- One-way push (portal -> Shopify); the app never deletes store products.
- Please don't delete the pre-configured source — use it for the sync test.
- The "Guide" button (top right) gives a 4-step overview.
